Who We Are

Built on Trust, Delivered on Time

Dantega Construction was founded on a simple idea: commercial owners deserve a contractor who tells them the truth about cost and schedule — and then keeps its word. We serve San Antonio and the surrounding region with full-service general contracting and pre-construction, handling projects for all types of clients across office, retail, medical, education, industrial, automotive, religious, and hospitality sectors.

The principal who prices your job runs your job. That means the assumptions behind the number are the same assumptions driving the field — no handoffs, no surprises, no scope gaps discovered at pay-app time.
