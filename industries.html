Industries We Serve

There is no project too small or too large. Dantega delivers commercial construction across eight sectors in San Antonio and the surrounding region.

Office

Corporate · Business · Tech

Class A finish-outs, corporate headquarters, professional suites, and tech workspaces — built for how your team actually works.

Retail

Shopping Centers · Restaurants · Storefronts

Retail centers, restaurant build-outs, and storefront renovations delivered fast, because every week closed is revenue lost.

Showrooms, service bays, and brand-standard dealership facilities that meet manufacturer image programs.

Schools · Universities · Training Facilities
