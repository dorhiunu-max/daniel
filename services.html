What We Do

Services

From pre-construction and commercial delivery to residential development and self-performed trades — one team, every phase, on budget and on schedule.

Before We Build

Pre-Construction

The best projects are won on paper first. Dantega's pre-construction services give owners a reliable number and a realistic schedule before a shovel hits the ground.

  • Estimating & budgeting — detailed, trade-by-trade pricing at every design stage
  • Value engineering — alternatives that cut cost without cutting quality
  • CPM scheduling — fully linked schedules with procurement and inspection logic built in
  • Permitting & approvals — city coordination and AHJ requirements handled early
  • Constructability review — catching conflicts in drawings before they become change orders
  • Bid packaging — qualified local subcontractors, competitively priced
In the Field

Construction

Once the plan is set, Dantega's field team takes over — self-managing the details that keep commercial projects moving from mobilization to Certificate of Occupancy.

  • Ground-up construction — new commercial buildings from sitework to finish-out
  • Tenant build-outs & finish-outs — office, retail, and medical interiors delivered turn-key
  • Renovation & remodel — occupied-space phasing that keeps your business running
  • Additions & expansions — growing your facility without disrupting it
  • MEP coordination — mechanical, electrical, and plumbing managed through startup and commissioning
  • Closeout & warranty — punch lists driven to zero, as-builts and O&Ms delivered, warranty honored
Beyond Commercial

Development & Residential

Dantega is more than a commercial GC — we develop land and build homes. From build-to-rent communities to one-of-a-kind custom builds, we bring the same schedule discipline to every rooftop.

  • Land development — site selection, entitlement, and horizontal development
  • Build-to-rent communities — design, construction, and turnover of BTR neighborhoods
  • Tract homes — production homebuilding at neighborhood scale
  • Custom homes — one-of-a-kind builds from your plans or ours
  • Landscape & maintenance — landscaping, irrigation, and ongoing property upkeep
Below the Ground

Utilities

Every project starts underground. Dantega installs the wet and dry utilities that serve our sites — and yours — with our own equipment and crews.

  • Wet utilities — water, sanitary sewer, and storm drainage
  • Dry utilities — gas, electric, and communications conduit
  • Excavation & trenching — mass excavation, trench safety, and compacted backfill
  • Taps & tie-ins — city and utility-provider coordination, inspections, and testing
  • Site drainage — grading, detention, and storm structures that keep sites dry
