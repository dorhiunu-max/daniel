What We Do

Services

Two disciplines, one team: plan the work in pre-construction, then deliver it in the field — on budget and on schedule.

Before We Build

Pre-Construction

The best projects are won on paper first. Dantega's pre-construction services give owners a reliable number and a realistic schedule before a shovel hits the ground.

  • Estimating & budgeting — detailed, trade-by-trade pricing at every design stage
  • Value engineering — alternatives that cut cost without cutting quality
  • CPM scheduling — fully linked schedules with procurement and inspection logic built in
  • Permitting & approvals — city coordination and AHJ requirements handled early
  • Constructability review — catching conflicts in drawings before they become change orders
  • Bid packaging — qualified local subcontractors, competitively priced
In the Field

Construction

Once the plan is set, Dantega's field team takes over — self-managing the details that keep commercial projects moving from mobilization to Certificate of Occupancy.

  • Ground-up construction — new commercial buildings from sitework to finish-out
  • Tenant build-outs & finish-outs — office, retail, and medical interiors delivered turn-key
  • Renovation & remodel — occupied-space phasing that keeps your business running
  • Additions & expansions — growing your facility without disrupting it
  • MEP coordination — mechanical, electrical, and plumbing managed through startup and commissioning
  • Closeout & warranty — punch lists driven to zero, as-builts and O&Ms delivered, warranty honored
