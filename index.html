Welcome to Dantega

Building San Antonio, One Project at a Time

Dantega Construction is a full-service commercial general contractor serving San Antonio and the surrounding region. We handle an array of building projects for all types of clients — offices, retail centers, medical facilities, schools, industrial spaces, and more.

  • Pre-construction — estimating, budgeting, value engineering, and scheduling
  • Construction — ground-up, renovation, and tenant build-out delivery
  • Owner-first communication — one point of contact from bid to closeout
  • Development & residential — land development, build-to-rent, tract and custom homes
  • Self-performed trades — concrete, framing, drywall, plumbing, electrical, HVAC, roofing, stucco, masonry, and flooring with our own crews
