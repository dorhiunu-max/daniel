## Role & technical

### 18. Walk me through how you handle a change order from field discovery to review with the project manager.
*From the job posting*

**Why they ask:** Straight from the posting: pricing, processing, and assessing cost and schedule impact before the PM sees it.

**Hit these from your resume:**
- Field discovery, photo, RFI or field directive, notice per contract
- Takeoff the delta, gather sub pricing, apply markups, check the schedule fragnet
- Log it, give the PM a one-page impact summary, track approval to the pay app

### 19. How do you review a subcontractor's submittal before forwarding it to the design team?
*From the job posting*

**Why they ask:** The posting wants compliance with scope, design documents, and coordination with other disciplines checked before the engineer sees it.

**Hit these from your resume:**
- Spec section checklist: product data, listing, ratings, dimensions against drawings
- Cross-discipline check: gear footprint vs. room, conduit routing vs. structure and piping
- Stamp, log, track review durations against the submittal schedule

### 20. Describe your RFI process. How do you keep RFIs from stalling the schedule?
*From the job posting*

**Hit these from your resume:**

- Weekly aging review with the design team; escalate the ones on the critical path
- Close the loop: distribute the answer, mark the drawings, update as-builts

### 21. How do you perform quantity takeoffs and use them to report progress? For electrical scope, what would you track?

## Safety & HSE

### 30. How familiar are you with construction safety standards, and how do you apply them day to day?
*PCL culture*

**Why they ask:** PCL will not compromise safety. This is your best category; land it without preaching.

**Hit these from your resume:**
- OSHA 510 and 30, EM 385-1-1, HAZWOPER, NFPA 70E, MSHA Part 46, First Aid/CPR

### 31. What would you do if you saw a subcontractor's crew working unsafely on your project?
*Standard coordinator question*

**Why they ask:** Tests whether you act in the moment and follow through in the system.

**Hit these from your resume:**
- Stop the work, remove the immediate hazard, talk to the foreman respectfully
- Document it, notify your superintendent and PCL safety, and track the corrective action to closure
- Follow-up: retraining, revised JHA, and a check that it stuck

### 32. How would you approach heat illness prevention on an industrial site in Chandler, Arizona?
*Likely from your resume*

**Why they ask:** Chandler summers exceed 110°F. Location-specific and personal to you; share only what you are comfortable sharing.

**Hit these from your resume:**
- Water, shade, rest cycles, acclimatization for new and returning workers, buddy system
- Schedule the heaviest work early; heat index triggers in the pre-task plan
- Someone trained to spot early signs and an emergency response plan on every crew
